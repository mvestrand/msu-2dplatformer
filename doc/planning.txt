Survival idea core loop
- Too much to do to be practical

 -> Each day in base player must fulfill basic needs
    - Improvements here improve expedition ability
    - Critical (will kill you if not quickly dealt with)
        - Food (hydroponics, greenhouse)
        - Water (water treatment)
        - Oxygen (life support)
        - Sleep (crew bunk)
        - Temperature (life support?)
    - Secondary (will cause serious problems if not dealt with)
        - Power & light (powerplant)
        - Security (blast doors?)
        - Health & sickness (medbay, radiation shielding)
        - Waste management (sewage, greenhouse)
        - Surface scanner?
    - Comfort (give you bonuses, but can survive without them)
        - Gym
        - Lounge
        - Lab
        - Bedroom
        - ?
 -> Expedition
    - Finds here allow base to be improved
    - Go to exploration sites to look for materials, find info, advance game, find blueprints
